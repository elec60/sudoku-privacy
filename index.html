Sudoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Sudoku has no accounts, and the game itself plays fully offline. The Google Play version uses the network only for crash reports and usage statistics (statistics with your consent where the law asks for it) and for two optional features: reward videos and the Supporter purchase.

The Cafe Bazaar version

The version of Sudoku from Cafe Bazaar has no reward videos, no usage statistics and no crash reports, and contains none of Google's services, so the sections about them below do not apply to it. It collects nothing. It uses the network only for the Supporter purchase, which Cafe Bazaar processes under its own terms; the developer receives no payment details.

Who is responsible

Mirhashem Mousavi Mehmandousti, the developer of Sudoku, is the controller of your data in the sense of the GDPR. See "Contact" below.

Stored on your device

Your progress (solved puzzles, streaks and records), your hint count, the themes you have unlocked, your settings (theme, language, sound, haptics, music and the game options), whether you own the Supporter upgrade and, in the Google Play version, your consent choices are stored locally on your device and never leave it. On Android they may be included in your personal device backup if you have backups enabled. Deleting the app removes them; "Reset progress" in Settings clears solves, streaks and records.

Your consent choices (Google Play version)

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the usage statistics and what is described under "Reward videos" do not happen until you have agreed to them. Crash reports do not depend on this choice. At launch the app asks Google whether consent is needed where you are, which transmits your IP address. The consent form appears the first time you choose to watch a reward video. Where the law asks for it, you can open it again at any time under Settings → "Privacy options", to agree, refuse or withdraw; refusing is as easy as agreeing and you keep the game and everything else in it. Videos may still be served as limited ads; where they are not, the routes that need a video (an extra hint, a second chance, opening a past daily puzzle) are unavailable and only the Supporter purchase remains. Withdrawing does not affect what was processed before.

Elsewhere, the features below are active from the first launch where local law allows it.

Usage statistics and crash reports (Google Play version)

The Google Play version contains Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, so that problems can be found and fixed and the game improved.

  • Analytics is switched off until you have agreed (or no consent is needed where you are). It records how the game is played: when a puzzle is started, won or lost (with the kind of game, difficulty, board, stars, how long it took, and whether a lost run was lost to mistakes or to the clock), when a hint is used, when a reward video is offered or watched, and where the Supporter upgrade was shown to you. It also records app opens, screens and session length, together with device model, system version, app version, language, approximate location derived from the IP address, and an app-instance identifier. It is used for advertising only if you also agreed to the advertising purposes.
  • Crashlytics sends crash reports from release builds of the app, whatever you chose above, because a crash can only be fixed if it is known. It records crashes: the stack trace, device model, system version, app version and an installation identifier. It is not used for advertising.

Both are provided by Google; see the Firebase privacy information. The iOS and desktop versions contain neither.

Reward videos (Google Play version)

There are no banners and no ads that interrupt play. You can choose to watch a video to get a hint, to continue a lost run or to open a past daily puzzle; if you never tap those buttons, no ad is shown.

The videos are served by Google AdMob. To load and show them, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects data such as your IP address (which gives an approximate location), the device's advertising ID, device and app information, and how you interact with an ad. Google uses this to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, and, where you allow it, to personalise ads. See how Google uses this data.

Supporter purchase

In the Google Play version the Supporter upgrade is bought through Google Play, which processes the payment; Sudoku never sees your payment details and only learns whether the upgrade is owned. In the Cafe Bazaar version Cafe Bazaar does the same. Supporters have unlimited hints, every theme and every past daily puzzle, and a second chance in a trial, all without a video, so no ad is shown to them.

Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Usage statistics and ads: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and the rules on storing or reading information on your device).
  • Crash reports: legitimate interest in a stable app (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object by writing to the address below.
  • Asking whether consent is needed, and remembering your answer: legal obligation and legitimate interest in meeting it (Art. 6(1)(c) and (f)).
  • The Supporter purchase: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).

Recipients and transfers

Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC process the data above as described in their policies. Data may be transferred to the United States and other countries; Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses for this.

How long data is kept

Analytics data tied to an identifier is kept for at most 14 months, crash reports for 90 days. Data on your device stays until you delete the app.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of its processing, a copy in a portable format, and you can object to processing and withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to your data protection authority. Because the app has no accounts, please include how we can find your data when you write to us; deleting the app and resetting your advertising ID in the system settings cuts the link to past data.

Children

Sudoku is not directed at children under 16, and no consent is asked of them knowingly.
